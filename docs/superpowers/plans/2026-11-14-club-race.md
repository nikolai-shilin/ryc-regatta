# Club Race Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An organiser sets up a race, shares a link, and club members sail it live together with computer boats making
up the fleet. The organiser's browser is the referee, and another member's browser takes over if it drops.

**Architecture:** `sim.js` learns to race several human boats, hand any boat to a computer skipper, and save and restore
the whole race. A new dependency-free Supabase Realtime client (`net.js`) and a DOM-free protocol module (`club.js`)
carry the waiting room, inputs, snapshots, events, handover and the final sheet. `club-ui.js` and small changes to
`main.js` and `world.js` put it on screen. Next.js pages add `/club/new`, `/club/<code>` and `/club/connect`. The
scores server (`iyt-test`) needs no change: it already takes a start and a result for any race id.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules in `public/game/` (no build step), Supabase Realtime over a raw WebSocket (Phoenix
JSON protocol v1), Next.js 16 App Router, `node:test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-club-race-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Work on a branch `club-race`, cut from `main` after the uncommitted changes there are committed by their owner.
- `public/game/` stays build-free ES modules. No `@supabase/*` package is loaded by the game; `net.js` speaks the
  protocol itself, and only a club race imports it (dynamic `import()`).
- The single-player game (today's race, free race) behaves exactly as before: the golden test from Task 1 must stay
  green after every task.
- Supabase project `iyt-test` (`xpdhkahgvusuicynssxq`), Pro plan: 500 messages/s, 500 connections, 50 presence msgs/s
  per project. Design budget: about 110 messages a second for six members, and under 200 for ten. Presence is not used
  at all.
- Rates: snapshots 10 Hz. A full race copy for handover every 3 s. Inputs on change, at most 10/s, and at least 2/s
  while idle. Waiting-room hello every 3 s, never during the race.
- Every user-facing string is in English and Russian (`t('…', '…')` on pages; the `T` tables in the game).
- Fleet size: 2 to 10 boats (`FLEET.length + 1`). Time limit: 600 s after the first boat finishes. Hold course after
  3 s without input; computer skipper after 10 s. Handover when no snapshot has come for 4 s. Start by anyone once the
  organiser has been absent from the waiting room for 120 s.
- A human boat that crosses the finish while the computer is sailing her is DNF. A boat still racing at the time
  limit is DNF.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` pass at the end of every task.

## Review Focus

1. **The organiser's phone goes to the background right after Start** (its animation frames stop). Expected: within
   about 5 s another member's game is the referee and the race carries on. When the organiser comes back, their game
   follows the new referee and doesn't fight it. → Task 6, `test: handover when the referee goes silent`.
2. **A member's tab is backgrounded mid-race, then comes back.** Expected: their boat holds course, then the computer
   sails her. On return they steer again, and their boat jumps to where the referee has her rather than where it was
   left. → Task 5, `test: a silent member holds course, then the computer sails her, then she is given back`.
3. **Members open the link before the organiser is there, or the organiser never returns.** Expected: "Waiting for the
   organiser". Start appears for everyone 120 s after the organiser was last seen. A member whose page loaded another
   venue reloads into the right one. → Task 4, `test: anyone may start once the organiser has been gone 120 s`.
4. **More members than boats, or someone joins after Start.** Expected: the first `fleet` members, by join time, sail.
   The rest, and anyone arriving after Start, watch. → Task 4, `test: the fleet takes members by join time, the rest
   watch`.
5. **Two members with the same name, or an empty name.** Expected: an empty name becomes "No name" / "Без имени", and a
   repeated name gets " 2", " 3" by join time, so the sheet tells them apart. → Task 4, `test: names are made
   unique`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `new Race({ ...existing, humans?: { id: string, name: string }[], me?: string | null, club?: boolean })`.
    Default `humans = [{ id: 'you', name: 'You' }]`, `me = humans[0].id`.
  - `race.humans: Boat[]`, in the order given. Each has `b.human = true`, `b.id`, `b.name`, `b.log` (the object that
    is `this.log` today) and `b.ai = null`. The first human takes today's player spot. Human *k* (k ≥ 1) takes the
    spot of computer boat *k* (`boats[k]`), whose `ai` is removed. The `rand()` draws are unchanged, so a one-human race
    is the same race as before.
  - `race.player`: the human whose `id === me`. With `me === null` (a watcher): `humans[0]`, and `race.watching = true`.
  - `race.log`: a getter, `this.player.log`.
  - `race.say(key, arg, to = null)` pushes `{ key, arg, to }`, where `to` is a Boat or `null` (for everyone).
    Every existing `b === this.player` branch becomes `b.human` with `to = b`.
  - `race.youCanHail(P = this.player)`, `race.hail(P = this.player)`. A hail at a human boat says `'hail'`
    `{ who: P.name, rule }` to her.
  - `hails()` runs for every human. In `drive`, the boat to cover is the nearest unfinished human on the same leg.
  - The chop in `step` follows the wind at the mean position of the unfinished humans (all humans, if none is
    unfinished).

### Task 2: Computer takeover, DNF, time limit and the member's own step

**Files:**
- Modify: `public/game/sim.js`
- Test: `test/sim.test.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `race.humans`, `b.human`.
- Produces:
  - `race.makeAi(b, i)`: today's per-boat `ai` object, factored out of the constructor without changing the order of
    `rand()` draws there.
  - `race.setComputer(b, on: boolean)`. On: `b.ai = race.makeAi(b, index)` with `skill: 0.85`, `b.autoTrim = true`,
    `b.computer = true` (it has been sailed by the computer at some point). Off: `b.ai = null` and `b.autoTrim` back to

  - In `progress`, a human finishing with `b.ai` set gets `b.dnf = true` and `place = null`.
  - With `club: true`: when the first boat finishes, `race.limitT = clock + 600`. After `limitT`, every boat not done

  - `race.stepView(dt, b)`: advances `clock`, `t`, the wind and the waves exactly as `step` does, and steps only `b`
    (`b.step(dt, this)`), plus her track. No `drive`, `progress`, rules or signals: on a member's screen those come from
    the referee.

### Task 4: The Realtime client and the waiting room

**Files:**
- Create: `public/game/net.js`, `public/game/club.js` (the `Lobby` part), `test/hub.mjs`, `test/net.test.mjs`,
  `test/club.test.mjs`, `scripts/realtime-smoke.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (`net.js`):
  - `realtime({ url, key, WebSocket = globalThis.WebSocket }) → { channel(topic, { self = false } = {}), close() }`.
  - `channel → { join(): Promise<void>, send(event, payload): void, on(event, fn(payload)): void, leave(): void }`.
  - Wire format: the socket at `${url with http→ws}/realtime/v1/websocket?apikey=${key}&vsn=1.0.0`. Frames are
    `{ topic: 'realtime:' + topic, event, payload, ref, join_ref }`. The join is `phx_join` with
    `{ config: { broadcast: { self, ack: false }, presence: { key: '', enabled: false }, postgres_changes: [], private: false }, access_token: key }`.
    Sending is event `broadcast` with `{ type: 'broadcast', event, payload }`. A heartbeat goes to topic `phoenix`
    (`heartbeat`) every 25 s. After a close, it reconnects after 1, 2, 5, then every 10 s, and rejoins every channel.
    A `send` before the join is answered is dropped.
- Produces (`club.js`):
  - `makeLink(rt, code, myId) → { all: Channel /* 'club:'+code */, inputOf(id): Channel /* 'club:'+code+':in:'+id */ }`.
  - `new Lobby({ link, me: { id, name, look }, organiser: boolean, settings: object | null, now = () => Date.now() })`
    - Sends `hello { id, name, look, joinedAt, organiser, settings }` on joining and every 3 s; sends `bye { id }` on
      `leave()`.
    - `lobby.roster(): { id, name, look, joinedAt, organiser, seenAt }[]`, sorted by `joinedAt` then `id`, dropping
      anyone unseen for 10 s.
    - `lobby.settings`: the first settings heard (the organiser's), or its own.
    - `lobby.canStart(): boolean`: true for the organiser; for anyone else, once no organiser has been seen for 120 s
      (counted from their own join if no organiser has ever been seen).
    - `lobby.start(): StartMsg`: broadcasts and returns
      `start { settings, seed, sheetId: uuid, refId: me.id, epoch: 1, humans: { id, name, look }[] }`. `humans` is the
      roster's first `settings.fleet` members, with names made unique (an empty name becomes the no-name string the
      lobby was given, and a repeated one gets " 2", " 3").
    - `lobby.onStart(fn(StartMsg))`. A member not in `humans` is a watcher.
- `test/hub.mjs`: `memoryRealtime()` → an object with the same `channel()` shape, delivering between every client made
  from the same hub, and honouring `self: false`.
- `lib/club.ts`: `CODE_ALPHABET = 'ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789'`, `newCode(): string` (6 characters),
  `CODE_RE = /^[A-HJ-KM-NP-Z2-9]{6}$/`, `ORG_KEY = 'regatta-club-org'` (sessionStorage: the code this tab organises).

### Task 5: Referee and followers

**Files:**
- Modify: `public/game/club.js`
- Test: `test/club.test.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `StartMsg` (Task 4), `Race` options and `setComputer`/`stepView`/`snapshot` (Tasks 1–3), `makeLink`.
- Produces:
  - `raceFor(start: StartMsg, myId: string | null): Race`: `new Race({ ...settings, seed, club: true, humans, me })`,
    with `me = myId` if they're among `humans`, else `null`. It also sets `b.look` and `b.color` (the hull colour) on
    each human.
  - `controlsOf(b) → { r, s, j, at, sp, k, kt, rf, sj, tk, hl }`: rudder, sheet, jib sheet, auto-trim, split, kite
    wanted, kite type, reef wanted, small jib wanted, tack count, hail count. The game bumps `b.tackN` and `b.hailN`
    when the button is pressed.
  - `applyControls(race, b, c)`: sets the rudder (unless `b.auto`) and the sheets. If `c.tk > b.tackSeen`, it calls
    `b.tackOrGybe()`. A kite, reef or jib that differs from what she has or is doing is changed through
    `setKite`/`setReef`/`setJib`. If `c.hl > b.hailSeen`, it calls `race.hail(b)`. It also sets
    `b.usedAuto ||= c.at && !b.ai` (sailed with auto-trim, for the sheet). The referee sets the same flag on its own
    boat each tick.
  - `new Referee({ race, link, start, epoch, now })`:
    - `tick(dt)`: applies the latest controls for each human, then runs the silence rules: after 3 s without input,
      rudder 0 and `auto = null` (hold course); after 10 s, `setComputer(b, true)`; on fresh input,
      `setComputer(b, false)`. Then `race.step(dt)`, and the sends: `snap` every 0.1 s, `state` every 3 s,
      `ev { list: [{ key, arg, to: id | null }] }` for each step's messages, and `sheet` once `race.over`.
    - `snap { e: epoch, c: clock, b: [...] }`: per boat, what `world.js` and the HUD read of another boat (position,
      heading, speed, heel, rudder, sheets, kite state, boom, leg, pen, ocs, done, dnf, place). The exact array layout is
      the builder's, built by `encodeSnap(race)` and read by `applySnap(race, s, skip: Boat)`.
    - It listens on `inputOf(id)` for every human except its own boat. The referee's own boat is driven by the local
      UI, as in a single-player race.
    - `stop()`: leaves the input channels, and sends nothing more.
  - `new Follower({ race, link, me: Boat | null, now })`:
    - `onSnap`: buffers snapshots with their arrival time. `frame(dt)`: draws the other boats at `now − 150 ms`,
      interpolated between the two snapshots around it; `race.stepView(dt, me)`; then eases `me` toward the
      referee's pose (a 0.5 s time constant), snapping at once if it's more than 20 m away, or after a `bump`, `aground`,
      `touched` or `fouled` event. The race clock follows the snapshots.
    - `input(controls)`: sends on `inputOf(me.id)` when the controls change, at most every 0.1 s, and at least every
      0.5 s.
    - `events → race.messages`, turning `to: id` into the Boat, and keeping only `to === me` or `to === null`.
    - `lastState`: the latest `state` message. It also records every boat's track at 1 Hz from the snapshots.

    heard from in the last 4 s plus itself, once a second, so every follower has the same list.
  - `new Session({ link, start, myId, now })`: owns the role. It starts as a `Referee` if `start.refId === myId`, else

      with `epoch + 1`, and sends
      `ref { id: myId, epoch }`.
    - Any game that hears a `snap` or `ref` with a higher epoch, or an equal epoch from a smaller `id`, while it is a
      referee, stops and becomes a follower of that one. An organiser who returns is just a follower.
    - `session.race`, `session.role: 'referee' | 'follower'`, `session.onRole(fn)` (the game swaps its loop on it).
